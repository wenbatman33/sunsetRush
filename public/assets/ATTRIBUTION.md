# Asset attribution

All files are distributed as free components of Sunset Rush.

- **Road sportbike**, Motorcycle Racing and Street Bikes by 3DAssets.dev — https://3dassets.dev/assets/motorcycle-racing-and-street-bikes-road-sportbike-e1418015 — CC0. Source https://cdn.3dassets.dev/assets/15424/v1/model.glb . Separate wheel and handlebar nodes used for riding animation; repainted red with clear coat, metal and rubber materials.
- **Superhero Male**, Universal Base Characters (Standard) by Quaternius — https://quaternius.com/packs/universalbasecharacters.html — CC0. Source archive https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-base-characters . Original mesh, skin weights and humanoid skeleton preserved. Bones posed for seated riding, fingers curled, suit recolored with red/graphite/cream materials, head faces hidden under helmet. Reproduction script: scripts/prepare-rider.py.
- **Car Kit**, **City Kit Roads**, **City Kit Suburban**, **Racing Kit** by Kenney — https://kenney.nl/assets — CC0. Traffic, barriers, road surface, road markings, arch, ramp, grass and railings. Road surface / line strip extracted from original triangles. Original licenses included. Remaining suburban road tile is used for the sidewalk.
- **Crayon Asset Library** — https://app.usecrayon.ai/assetlibrary#license — buildings, trees, street lamp, bench, gold coin, coastal palm. Free for personal and commercial games and interactive projects; modification and free redistribution permitted; selling standalone assets prohibited. Original collider meshes removed from render containers. Selected model names match local filenames.
- **Full-face helmet on stand**, Motorcycle Racing and Street Bikes — https://3dassets.dev/packs/motorcycle-racing-and-street-bikes — CC0. Stand removed; helmet scaled and attached to rider, existing surface normals smoothed without adding geometry, shell and visor materials refined. Source https://cdn.3dassets.dev/assets/15435/v1/model.glb .
- **Sea Water Module** and **Beach Shingle Module**, Greek Island Village and Harbour — https://3dassets.dev/packs/greek-island-village-and-harbour — CC0. Sources https://cdn.3dassets.dev/assets/37100/v1/model.glb and https://cdn.3dassets.dev/assets/37080/v1/model.glb .
- **Asphalt 02** and **Kloofendal 48d Partly Cloudy Pure Sky**, Poly Haven — https://polyhaven.com/a/asphalt_02 and https://polyhaven.com/a/kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky — CC0. Used as PBR textures (diffuse, normal, roughness) and HDR illumination / sky.

No Temple Run artwork, characters, logos or audio are included.

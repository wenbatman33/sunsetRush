# Asset attribution

All files are distributed as free components of Sunset Rush.

- **Motorcycle** by Poly by Google — https://poly.pizza/m/dse64pqMKAR — CC BY 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Original file retained as street-motorcycle-source.glb. Geometry and palette retained; wheel components separated, scale/orientation normalized, normals recalculated, and windshield/headlight materials separated. Reproduction script: scripts/prepare-motorcycle.py.
- **Full-face helmet revision** — custom generic model following the user-supplied full-face helmet silhouette reference; no manufacturer logo or artwork copied. Continuous curved shell and flush visor, chin guard, bottom lining and ventilation marks. This replaces the rejected spherical helmet. Reproduction script: scripts/prepare-fullface.py.
- **Superhero Male**, Universal Base Characters (Standard) by Quaternius — https://quaternius.com/packs/universalbasecharacters.html — CC0. Source archive https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-base-characters . Original mesh, skin weights and humanoid skeleton preserved. Bones posed to saddle, handlebar and footrest targets, fingers curled and hand proportions reduced, suit recolored red/graphite using blended vertex colors, head faces hidden under helmet. Reproduction script: scripts/prepare-rider.py.
- **Car Kit**, **City Kit Roads**, **City Kit Suburban**, **Racing Kit** by Kenney — https://kenney.nl/assets — CC0. Traffic, barriers, road surface, road markings, arch, ramp, grass and railings. Road surface / line strip extracted from original triangles. Original licenses included. Remaining suburban road tile is used for the sidewalk.
- **Crayon Asset Library** — https://app.usecrayon.ai/assetlibrary#license — buildings, trees, street lamp, bench, gold coin, coastal palm. Free for personal and commercial games and interactive projects; modification and free redistribution permitted; selling standalone assets prohibited. Original collider meshes removed from render containers. Selected model names match local filenames.
